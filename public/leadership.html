Board of Directors

Anthony Nwachukwu Nwokoro

CHAIRMAN / CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER (CEO)

A distinguished construction, infrastructure, and energy executive whose career of over 25 years reflects an unwavering commitment to strategic leadership, operational excellence, and measurable commercial impact.

MoreLess

Anthony Nwachukwu Nwokoro is a distinguished construction, infrastructure, and energy executive whose career of over 25 years reflects an unwavering commitment to strategic leadership, operational excellence, and measurable commercial impact. Across boardrooms, construction sites, and energy facilities, he has consistently demonstrated the ability to translate complex challenges into tangible outcomes, earning his place among the most respected figures in Nigeria’s built environment and energy sectors.

At the core of Anthony’s corporate career is his role as Executive Director and Chief Operating Officer of Stefanutti Stocks Hapel Ltd, a high-profile joint venture between Stefanutti Stocks SA, one of South Africa’s foremost publicly listed construction groups, and Hapel Nigeria Limited. In this capacity, he carries full responsibility for strategic direction, operational performance, and corporate governance across the company’s Nigerian operations. His most defining contribution remains his role as Chief Negotiator in the landmark merger between Stefanutti Stocks SA and Hapel Nigeria Limited, a cross-border, multi-jurisdictional transaction that he led from strategy to conclusion with exceptional commercial precision and stakeholder diplomacy. Under his leadership, the company has overseen project portfolios exceeding USD 20 million, directed a workforce of over 500 employees, and secured high-value contracts across government, parastatal, and private sector segments.

Anthony also serves as the Chairman / Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of Verte Energies Limited, a fully indigenous Nigerian energy and engineering company headquartered in Abuja, incorporated under CAMA 2020 and fully compliant with the Nigerian Oil and Gas Industry Content Development (NOGICD) Act. The company delivers integrated solutions across oil and gas services, engineering, procurement, construction and maintenance (EPCM), civil and structural engineering, marine inspection, and laboratory testing, operating in accordance with ISO 9001, ISO 14001, and ISO 45001 standards. At Verte Energies, Anthony leads with a governance model built on transparency, ethical conduct, and a long-term vision to become a pan-African brand synonymous with engineering excellence, innovation, and sustainability.

Beyond his corporate roles, Anthony is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Ver-amount Construction Ltd, a luxury residential development and estate construction firm serving Nigeria’s premium property market, and of Tile Gallery Nigeria Ltd, a specialist enterprise focused on the sourcing, supply, and contracting of premium natural stone and luxury tiling products for high-end residential, commercial, and hospitality projects. Together, these ventures reflect his deep understanding of the construction value chain and his ability to build complementary businesses of lasting commercial value.

Anthony holds a Bachelor’s degree in Management from the University of Nigeria, Nsukka. His academic grounding, combined with decades of applied executive experience across multinational construction and indigenous energy enterprises, has shaped a leader of exceptional breadth. He is a strategist, a negotiator, and a builder in every sense, whose contributions to Nigeria’s infrastructure and energy landscape continue to define the standard for executive leadership in the sector.

A highly accomplished professional known for his transformative leadership, technical excellence and passion for continuous improvement with a proven track record of delivering large-scale, high-impact complex capital projects, including first-of-their-kind developments from concept selection through execution. His leadership is defined by a focus on operational excellence, asset integrity and value optimisation across the project lifecycle.

Olusayo is a trailblazer in technological innovations and has held multiple senior leadership roles within Shell E&P Company, Oryx Gas to Liquids and Foundation Edge Inc. He brings deep expertise in front-end development, field development planning, engineering management, capital project execution planning & management, risk & opportunity management, independent project reviews & assurance and asset management. Olusayo holds a Doctor of Philosophy (Hon) in Project Leadership & Engineering Management from AMU in USA, an MBA from University of Liverpool in UK, a Postgraduate Certificate in Oil & Gas Engineering from Shell/Robert Gordon University, Aberdeen, Scotland training scheme and a Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) in Mechanical Engineering from Obafemi Awolowo University in Nigeria.

Olusayo is deeply committed to mentoring, talent development and building high-performing teams. He is actively involved in industry associations, serving as board member to promote corporate governance in some business entities and on global professional accreditation panels contributing to engineering and project management discourse.

He is a member of several professional bodies such as Nigerian Society of Engineers (NSE), Association of Project Management (APM) in UK, Association of Professional Engineers and Geoscientists of Alberta (APEGA) in Canada, Project Management Institute (PMI) in USA, Project Management Association of Canada (PMAC), American Society of Engineering Management (ASEM), American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME) and Chartered Management Institute (CMI) in UK.

He has been acknowledged with several recognitions and professional awards. His recent accolade in 2026 includes the Most Influential Leader Driving Innovation in Global Oil & Gas from the Empire Magazine.

Collins Okaru

GENERAL MANAGER (TECHNICAL)

A Chartered Engineer, PMP, and energy leader with over 20 years of experience delivering offshore, subsea, infrastructure, and industrial automation projects worldwide.

MoreLess

Collins Okaru is an accomplished project and engineering leader with extensive international experience delivering complex capital projects across the global energy industry. Over the course of his career, he has held key engineering and project management roles supporting major international operators and engineering consultancies, including Shell, TotalEnergies, Xodus Group, the defunct Nigerian Agip Oil Company, TSKJ/JBN and NPSG Global. His expertise spans the complete project lifecycle—from opportunity maturation, concept selection, feasibility studies and Front-End Engineering Design (FEED) through detailed engineering, procurement, offshore execution, commissioning, start-up and operational readiness.

Recognised for his strategic leadership and technical excellence, Collins has successfully led multidisciplinary teams responsible for delivering high-value offshore and subsea developments across Africa, Europe, Asia, and the Middle East. His professional experience encompasses subsea production systems, SURF developments, offshore pipeline, riser and umbilical installations, brownfield modifications, asset integrity, engineering assurance, project execution planning, project controls, contract administration, risk and opportunity management, stakeholder engagement and capital project governance.

Throughout his career, he has consistently demonstrated the ability to deliver technically challenging projects safely, efficiently and within commercial objectives while maintaining the highest standards of quality and operational excellence. Collins is widely respected for integrating engineering expertise with commercial insight to optimise project performance, strengthen governance, improve execution efficiency, and create sustainable value for clients and stakeholders. His leadership philosophy is founded on integrity, accountability, innovation, collaboration, and the development of high-performing multidisciplinary teams. He is a passionate advocate for indigenous capacity development and the transfer of global best practices to strengthen Africa’s engineering and energy sectors.

He is a Chartered Engineer (CEng) registered with the UK Engineering Council, a Chartered Petroleum Engineer with the Energy Institute (United Kingdom), and a Registered Engineer with the Council for the Regulation of Engineering in Nigeria (COREN). He is also a certified Project Management Professional (PMP), reflecting his commitment to internationally recognised standards in engineering and project delivery. Collins holds a Master of Science (MSc) in Offshore and Ocean Technology (Subsea Engineering) from Cranfield University, United Kingdom; a Master’s degree in Project Management and a Bachelor of Engineering (B.Eng.) in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from the Federal University of Technology, Owerri (FUTO).

His multidisciplinary academic background complements his extensive practical experience in delivering major energy infrastructure projects. As General Manager (Technical) at Verte Energies Limited, Collins provides strategic leadership for the Company’s engineering, project delivery and business growth initiatives. He is responsible for strengthening project delivery standards, governance, advancing engineering capability, and expanding strategic partnerships to position Verte as partner of choice, globally respected technology-driven indigenous engineering company delivering integrated energy and infrastructure solutions throughout Africa and beyond.

Samson Onoh

GENERAL MANAGER (PROJECTS AND OPERATIONS PORTFOLIO)

A COREN-registered engineer and PMP-certified portfolio leader managing projects, operations, delivery priorities, and performance for Verte Energies.

MoreLess

Samson Onoh is a seasoned, APM certified Project Practitioner with over 30 years industry experience in facilities development, Engineering design and Construction. He led several multidiscipline project teams to deliver major greenfield and brownfield opportunities across numerous Assets in the shallow and deep offshore fields of Nigeria. He started his career as a project engineer for PLS Nacap International (JV with B+B Oil & Gas Nigeria Ltd) where he delivered major oil and gas production facilities, flowlines, delivery lines and trunklines across key oil and gas fields in the East and West of Nigeria.

Samson then joined Shell as an experienced Mechanical Project Engineer with the Bonga Asset Engineering and Construction team. He helped the team to complete the rigorous post first oil Project-to-Asset handover and led various project teams to liquidate key post project handover legacy project over the years making the Bonga Asset a great investment case. Samson spent some time with the Static Mechanical Engineering Discipline team of Shell Nigeria Deep Water Projects Organization where he functioned for several years as a technical authority TA-Static Mechanical – Piping and valves. He later moved to the Front-End Engineering team where he managed all pre-DG3 opportunities required to address key Asset integrity threats, risks and opportunities on Bonga FPSO Topsides. His key role was the development and delivery of engineering solutions for Materials Projects, Major/Minor Projects designed to resolve asset integrity issues and systems obsolescence for Bonga FPSO. He later became the Portfolio Project Manager and the custodian of the SNEPCo Brownfield Portfolio Project Execution & Management Manual and also managed the DWP Project Maturation funnel.

Samson is known for his snag for efficient execution, with consistent eye on Projects/ Organizational score cards around cost and delivery timelines. He became the go to person for delivery of constructability reviews across Shell companies in Nigeria with a mantra; ‘Measure twice and cut once’.

As GM (Projects & Operations Portfolio) for Verte Energies, Samson provides strategic leadership for technical evaluation, planning and execution of business opportunities in Nigerian Oil and Gas sector. He leads a highly competent team that identifies, screens and delivers unique engineering solutions, strengthening Verte Energies’ competitive position in the market.
