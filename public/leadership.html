Collins Okaru

CHARTERED ENGINEER & PROJECT MANAGEMENT PROFESSIONAL

A Chartered Engineer, PMP, and energy leader with over 20 years of experience delivering offshore, subsea, infrastructure, and industrial automation projects worldwide.

MoreLess

Collins Okaru is an experienced Chartered Engineer with the Engineering Council of the United Kingdom, a Project Management Professional, and an energy industry leader with over 20 years of experience delivering complex offshore, subsea, energy, infrastructure, and industrial automation projects across North America, Europe, Africa, and the Gulf of Mexico.

His career includes leadership roles with major operators and engineering organisations including Shell, TotalEnergies, Xodus Group, and NPSG Global. He has extensive involvement in deepwater subsea developments covering Subsea Production Systems, SURF, controls and distribution systems, fabrication, commissioning, and project execution.

He has also led large-scale warehouse automation and e-fulfilment infrastructure programmes supporting Amazon fulfilment operations across North America and Europe, combining engineering excellence with strategic project delivery and operational optimisation.
