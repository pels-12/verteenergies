Services

Energy and engineering services for project delivery.

Verte Energies supports clients across energy, infrastructure, procurement, fabrication, field operations, testing, and compliance-driven project execution.

Oil & Gas Services

Pipeline construction, inspection, surveillance, integrity management, corrosion protection, safety and process control equipment, facility design, commissioning, gas distribution infrastructure, and oil field logistics.

EPCM & Project Management

Development concept engineering, project execution planning, capital project management, independent reviews, FEED and detailed engineering management, procurement, maintenance, refurbishment, and facility upgrades.

Civil & Structural Engineering

Feasibility studies, earthworks, piling, foundation construction, environmental and flood management systems, reinforced concrete, steel structures, roads, bridges, culverts, and drainage networks.

Fabrication & Mechanical Works

Steel structure fabrication, onsite assembly, structural modification, mechanical integrity restoration, piping, rigging, equipment alignment, tank cleaning, testing, coating, and pressure vessel works.

Procurement & Technical Support

Certified equipment, instruments, spare parts, global sourcing, vendor coordination, transport logistics, warehousing, quality assurance, inspection, documentation, and certification management.

Marine Audit & Inspection

Marine audits, vetting, ISM/ISPS inspections, cargo quality control, supercargo services, vessel condition and valuation surveys, draft surveys, hull and machinery inspections, bunker and discharge surveys.

Laboratory & Testing Services

Chemical composition and performance analysis, petroleum product quality testing, environmental sampling, contamination control, and research and development in petroleum chemistry.

Oil, gas, engineering, procurement, inspection, and infrastructure solutions delivered with safety, quality, and accountability.
